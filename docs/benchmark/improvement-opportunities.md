# Improvement Opportunities

These are product opportunities, not claims about the benchmark:

- Keep round state, bet lock, claim, result and settlement in one Round Centre.
- Show simulation-credit and no-cash status at every balance surface.
- Make the ledger reference and fairness seed inspectable before trust is requested.
- Treat cancellation, delay and dispute states as first-class UI states.
- Separate social referral progress from financial language.
